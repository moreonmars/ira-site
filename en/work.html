ABOUT THE WORK / DRAFT

Archive: Expedition considers the body as a moving archive — an unstable repository of gestures, experience and collective memory.

The project brings together solo, duo and group performances. Through repetition, physical exertion and interaction with space, the work asks what remains in the body after an event.

This text is a temporary editorial draft and will later be replaced by the artist’s own description.

“THE BODY REMEMBERS THROUGH MOVEMENT, PRESSURE AND REPETITION.”
